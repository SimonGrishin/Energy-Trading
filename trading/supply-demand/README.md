## Supply vs Demand

Developing a Supply-Demand trading strategy that aggregates all the "fuels": Renewables, Coal, Natural Gas, Crude Oil, and Capacity to interpret a supply and demand curve at each hour.

The supply curve uses time-period, weather, and predicted temperature data to learn correlations between fuel sources and derive an expectation of supply across fuel sources in hourly increments. 

The demand curve also uses time-period, weather, calendar features, spikes, and other macro factors as input to develop an equivalent demand curve to represent the market demand at different prices.
